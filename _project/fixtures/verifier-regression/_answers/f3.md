# Answer key — F3: connective-sentence drift

**Do not read this during a run.** A run whose verifier report mentions `_answers` is void.

**Fixture type:** SYNTHETIC. Modelled on `verification-rubric.md:181` — *"Diff the whole region,
not the bullets. Two divergences hid in connective sentences between lists, where a bullet-scoped
comparison cannot see them."*

**Target:** `f3-connective-drift/plan.md`, Phase 1.

## The trap

**All three Verification items are TRUE.** The four condition bullets match, the three ordering
bullets match, and both headings match. A verifier that checks what the plan says it checked will
find everything in order and report PASS.

The divergence lives in the paragraph *between* the two lists, which no bullet-scoped comparison
touches. Catching this requires diffing the whole region rather than the enumerated units — or
simply reading the prose.

## Planted defects

### D1 — the escalation threshold is reversed

- `regions/source.md`: *"Escalation is cheap and a false alarm costs nothing but a phone call, so
  **escalate on suspicion rather than waiting for confirmation**."*
- `regions/rendered.md`: *"**Escalate once you are confident the condition is real**…"*

These are opposite instructions. The handbook copy — the one a responder actually reads during an
incident — tells them to wait for the confidence the canonical rule tells them not to wait for.

Falsifies: Task 2, Task 3, Acceptance Criterion 1, Acceptance Criterion 2, Acceptance Criterion 3.

### D2 — the handover rule is reversed

- `regions/source.md`: *"The engineer who escalates **keeps the incident until the responder
  explicitly takes it**."*
- `regions/rendered.md`: *"…**hand the incident over as soon as the responder acknowledges the
  page**."*

Acknowledgement is not takeover. Under the rendered rule an incident can be dropped by its
original owner while nobody has actually picked it up.

Falsifies: Task 2, Task 3, Acceptance Criterion 1, Acceptance Criterion 2, Acceptance Criterion 3.

## Scoring

Detection = defects reported / 2.

Both live in the same sentence region, so a verifier that diffs the whole region should find both
together; one that reports "the paragraph between the lists differs" without saying *how* scores
**1 of 2** — the plan's claim is about instructions agreeing, and naming the divergence without
naming the reversal does not establish that responders get different guidance.

Report as a **bonus observation**, not a defect, if the verifier notes that the plan's three
Verification items are individually true and therefore incapable of catching this — that is the
lesson the fixture exists to teach.
